About the salon

Tucked into Priory Road, built on repeat clients.

The Nail Gallery and Beauty is a woman-owned salon on Priory Road in Bodmin, run by a small team of five who between them cover nails, facials, waxing, massage and more. It's a wheelchair-accessible space with parking nearby — easy to get to, easy to come back to.

That's exactly what Bodmin seems to be doing: this is one of the highest-reviewed salons anywhere in the area, almost entirely on the strength of word of mouth.
